A complete job application package

Ultimate Job Assistant is a research-driven, agentic toolkit that runs inside Claude. It decodes the job, researches the company, targets your resume, scores the match, drafts your "why this company" pitch, coaches a portfolio piece, finds warm introductions, and ships a deployable interview-prep study site for the role. Every step is human-in-the-loop. Nothing fabricated.

What it does, in one chain

For each role you target, the orchestrator walks you through eleven steps. Required steps run automatically; optional steps each get an explicit "yes / no / skip" prompt before executing.

Required path

  1. 01
    Setup
    Identifies company and role from the JD; sets the naming convention.
  2. 02
    Decoded JD
    Explicit requirements, implicit expectations, confidence-tagged.
  3. 04
    Resume targeter
    Deep company research, adaptive Q&A, bullet rewrite with your approval.
  4. 06
    "Why this company"
    Speaking points grounded in the research brief.
  5. 10
    Wrap-up
    Tracker update, doc-freshness pass.

Optional steps

  1. 03/05
    Resume scoring
    Before / after match scores against the decoded JD.
  2. 07
    Cover letter
    Drafted from the speaking points.
  3. 08
    Portfolio piece
    Scope, source data, plan, execute, package. The "dream company" investment.
  4. 09
    Networking outreach
    Find contacts, draft warm-intro messages.
  5. 9.5
    Interview-prep PWA — new in v0.1.0
    A deployable study site with worked examples, faded scaffolds, and free-recall problems. Sourced real questions from Glassdoor / Blind / Levels.fyi. Installable on your phone.

The interview-prep PWA

Built on a fixed pedagogical loop: worked example → faded scaffold → free recall, plus interleaving and spacing across topics. Modeled on the proven Netflix Interview Prep build that this project is forked from.

Came

Read the worked example

Three solutions per topic at increasing difficulty. Every walkthrough explains why a construct was chosen, not just what it does.

Saw

Fill in the blanks

Same problems, but with strategic blanks at error-prone tokens (join type, NULL operator, window-function choice). The scaffolding fades.

Conquered

Write it from scratch

New problems, blank editor, no scaffolding. Self-explain prompts after every attempt.

Citations live in the footer of every generated PWA.

The pedagogy draws on Sweller & Cooper (1985), Renkl (2014), Atkinson et al. (2003), Roediger & Karpicke (2006), Rohrer (2012), Cepeda et al. (2006), Paivio (1971), Mayer (2009), and Chi et al. (1994). No vibes-based study advice; every claim is sourced.

Three things keep this honest

No fabrication

Every claim about a company or interview process cites a credible source. Worked examples may use synthetic data; reported real questions in the "Real Qs" tab cite Glassdoor, Blind, Levels.fyi, or official posts.

Human-in-the-loop

Every skill checkpoints with you before producing output. Bullet rewrites, content drafts, networking messages — you approve everything before it's saved or sent.

Specific over vague

Resume bullets carry numbers, tools, outcomes, scope. Worked examples carry business motivation, not just mechanical asks. Cheap, generic content gets discarded.

Get started

The setup walkthrough takes about 30–45 minutes, mostly because the most valuable input — your memory.md — is worth thinking through carefully.

  1. 1.
    Clone the repo.
    Get the public companion repo onto your machine.
  2. 2.
    Personalize memory.md.
    Identity, work style, anchor stories, formatting preferences. Most important file in the project.
  3. 3.
    Drop in your base resume.
    DOCX preferred. Goes in base-resumes/.
  4. 4.
    Open Claude Desktop in Cowork mode.
    Select the project folder. Say "I want to apply to [Company] for the [Role]." The orchestrator takes it from there.
