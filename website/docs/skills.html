Reference

Skills

Eight skills, chained by the orchestrator. Each one is standalone and can also be invoked directly. Click into any one for the full SKILL.md spec on GitHub.

1 · Required spine

Orchestrator

The workflow engine. Chains all the other skills into a guided application process. You say "I want to apply to Stripe" and it walks you through every step from JD decoding to outreach, asking at each decision point.

Reads: every output folder. Writes: tracker.md updates (proposed, never auto).

2 · Required

Decoded JD

Two-pass JD analysis. Extracts explicit requirements (responsibilities, qualifications) and implicit expectations (what's between the lines: tools they don't name but clearly use, soft skills they imply). Tags each finding with confidence.

Reads: the JD URL or pasted text. Writes: decoded-jds/[convention].md

3 · Required

Resume Targeter

The deepest skill. Researches the company across culture, values, interview process, and role context. Conducts an adaptive Q&A to extract your strongest stories. Generates targeted bullets, walks you through change-review, then produces the final DOCX and PDF.

Reads: decoded JD, base resume, memory.md. Writes: research/[company].md, resumes/[convention].docx + .pdf

4 · Optional

Resume Scorer

Scores a resume against a decoded JD with category weights and per-category evidence. Run it before targeting (to set a baseline) and after (to see the delta). The scorer is the only skill that produces a single number, and it's deliberately one of many signals.

Reads: decoded JD, resume. Writes: scores/[convention]-before.md / -after.md

5 · Required (speaking points) + optional (cover letter)

Why This Company

Speaking points and optional cover letter, grounded in the research brief. Speaking points are a cheat sheet, not a script — designed to be glanced at, not read aloud. The cover-letter draft is generated only on request.

Reads: decoded JD, research brief, memory.md, targeted resume. Writes: speaking-points/[convention].md + .pdf, optionally cover-letters/[convention].md + .pdf

6 · Optional · the "dream company" investment

Portfolio Coach

Recommends, scopes, sources data for, and walks you through building a portfolio piece tailored to a target role. Coaches you; doesn't do the work for you. Project types include Tableau dashboards, Python/SQL analyses, written case studies, and AI/ML projects.

Reads: decoded JD, score reports, memory.md. Writes: portfolio/[convention]/

7 · Optional

Networking Intros

Finds plausible warm-intro paths and drafts the message. Narrow scope by design — does not run cold-outreach scrapes; does not maintain a CRM. Just helps you draft one good message at a time.

Reads: research brief, memory.md. Writes: networking/contacts.md, networking/outreach/

8 · Optional · NEW in v0.1.0

Interview Prep

Builds a deployable single-file Progressive Web App for the specific interview. Six phases: Inputs → Format inference → Topic synthesis → Content authoring → Real-Qs research → Build and ship. Pulls topics and vocabulary from your decoded JD and research; sources real reported questions from Glassdoor / Blind / Levels.fyi (no fabrication).

Reads: decoded JD, research brief, scorer report. Writes: interview-prep/[convention]-format.md, [convention]-content.json, [convention]-pwa/

How they fit together

The orchestrator runs them in this order, prompting before every optional step:

Step 1   Setup & Folder Creation         (required)
Step 2   Decoded JD                       (required)
Step 3   Resume Score (before)            (optional)
Step 4   Resume Targeter                  (required)
Step 5   Resume Score (after)             (auto if Step 3 was yes)
Step 6   Why This Company speaking points (required)
Step 7   Cover Letter                     (optional)
Step 8   Portfolio Project                (optional)
Step 9   Networking & Warm Intros         (optional)
Step 9.5 Interview Prep PWA               (optional, new)
Step 10  Wrap-up & Doc Freshness          (required)
