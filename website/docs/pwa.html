Interview-prep PWA

A deployable single-file study site, generated for one specific interview, built on a fixed pedagogical loop and sourced research. Modeled on the proven Netflix Interview Prep build that this project forks from.

The three-phase loop

For every topic, the PWA presents content in a fixed sequence. You move through them in order. By the end, you've written the query three times, each time with less help.

Phase 1 · Came

Read the worked example

Three solutions per topic at increasing difficulty. Schema panel, full SQL or Python solution, six-to-ten-bullet walkthrough that explains every choice. After the first worked example, an italic prompt to self-explain in your own words before moving on.

Phase 2 · Saw

Fill in the blanks

Same three problems. Strategic blanks at error-prone tokens (join type, NULL operator, window-function choice, threshold literals). The full solution is one tap away, but the value comes from struggling first.

Phase 3 · Conquered

Write it from scratch

Three new problems. Schema panel only; blank editor; no scaffolding. Your attempt persists to localStorage so you can close the tab and pick up later. Self-explain prompt after every attempt.

Two modes

Learn mode

Sequential, topic-by-topic. You work through each topic's three phases before moving to the next. Good for your first pass.

Interleave mode

Randomized prompts across all topics. Forces context-switching, which is what the real interview will require. Feels harder; works better. Use it once topics feel solid.

The Real Questions tab

Reported real interview questions for the company, sourced from credible places: Glassdoor (with date and role), Blind threads, Levels.fyi, official tech blogs. Every question carries its source URL and a date. The skill never invents questions; if credible sources turn up fewer than three, the tab renders an honest empty state.

For high-frequency patterns (questions that surface in three or more sources), the skill may write a hand-crafted solution — but flags it as solution_authored_by: claude so you can tell which solutions came from a candidate's report and which the AI wrote.

The pedagogy is sourced

Every PWA renders these citations in its footer. The pedagogy is not made up.

  • Worked examples: Sweller & Cooper (1985); Renkl (2014)
  • Fading: Atkinson, Renkl & Merrill (2003)
  • Retrieval practice: Roediger & Karpicke (2006)
  • Interleaving: Rohrer (2012); Taylor & Rohrer (2010)
  • Spacing: Cepeda et al. (2006)
  • Dual coding: Paivio (1971); Mayer (2009)
  • Elaboration: Chi et al. (1994)

How to deploy

Each generated PWA is a folder. Two clicks to a public URL:

  1. Open Netlify Drop.
  2. Drag the folder onto the page.
  3. Get an HTTPS URL.

No account, no credit card, no monthly cost.

Install on your phone

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the deployed URL.
  2. Tap the share button.
  3. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen".

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the deployed URL.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Tap "Install app".

After install the site loads instantly and works offline. Progress persists to localStorage in the browser; iOS Safari may clear it after about seven days inactive, so daily use keeps your work safe.
