# Resume Scoring Methodology
# Used by: resume-scorer skill

---

## Three-Dimension Blended Score

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| Keyword Coverage | 30% | How many JD keywords/terms appear in the resume |
| Qualification Match | 40% | How well the resume demonstrates each must-have and nice-to-have |
| Values Alignment | 30% | How well the resume signals cultural fit with the company's stated values |

## Rating Scale

- **Strong**: Clear, specific evidence in the resume
- **Partial**: Implicit or indirect evidence
- **Gap**: No evidence found

## Qualification Weighting

- Must-haves: weighted 2x
- Nice-to-haves: weighted 1x

## Values Scoring

- Aligned: 100 points
- Partial: 50 points
- Absent: 0 points
- Score = total points / max possible points
