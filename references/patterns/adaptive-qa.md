# Adaptive Q&A Pattern
# Used by: resume-targeter, why-this-company

---

## Structure

### Round 1: Core Experience Mapping
3-5 questions mapping user's experience to the role's primary responsibilities:
- Technical skills the role requires (with examples at scale)
- Cross-functional collaboration and stakeholder management
- Both fast-turnaround and strategic/long-term work

### Round 2: Company Values Alignment
3-5 questions surfacing experiences that map to the company's specific values:
- For each value, craft a question that pulls out a relevant story
- Example: if the company values "courage," ask about pushing back on a flawed assumption

### Subsequent Rounds (if needed)
Continue asking if:
- Key job requirements still have no supporting experience
- Answers were thin on specifics (numbers, tools, outcomes)
- There are company values with no corresponding story yet

## Confidence Tagging

Tag all findings that are inferred rather than explicitly stated:
- **[HIGH]**: Strong evidence from multiple sources
- **[MED]**: Reasonable inference from limited data
- **[LOW]**: Speculative, based on patterns rather than direct evidence

## Key Principles

- Never ask more than 3-5 questions per round
- Acknowledge and build on previous answers
- If user says "not sure," offer to inspire with a relevant angle
- Stories from memory.md can be referenced to prompt deeper detail
- Stop when you have enough material for strong, specific bullets
