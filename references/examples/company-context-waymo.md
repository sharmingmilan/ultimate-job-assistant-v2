## Company Profile

Waymo is Alphabet's autonomous driving subsidiary, spun out of the Google Self-Driving Car Project (2009). Mission: "Be the world's most trusted driver." 14+ million rider-only trips in 2025, ~3,000 vehicles across 10 US metro areas, $126B valuation. Core cultural signals: safety as identity, shared purpose (38,000 US traffic deaths/year as motivation), intellectual curiosity, data-driven decision-making. Alphabet-level benefits with startup-level mission.
